Each layer has its own broadphase and update rate, and the static layers are not touched by the per-step update. Take every approach that buys performance and efficiency and record each one, with its measured effect, in docs/ENGINE.md: layer-specific broadphase, no per-step work on the static layers, reused buffers, fixed pools, SIMD, the job system across host cores, and the minimal body count per tier.
